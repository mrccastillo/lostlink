## Why

LostLink accepts reports and private ownership claims, but staff can only see pending counts and recent reports. Staff need a way to publish a real listing, decide who can collect it, and record its return.

## What Changes

- Add paginated staff queues for reports, claims, and reserved items.
- Connect publication decisions to Discover and member-visible case statuses.
- Add private evidence review, exclusive reservations, cancellation, physical handoff, and per-case audit history.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `admin-case-management` covers the staff workflow from submission through return.

### Modified Capabilities

The repository has no archived OpenSpec capabilities yet.

## Impact

Affects the admin dashboard, Discover, My Posts, My Claims, private media access, and MongoDB report, claim, return, and audit records. The [Admin MVP specification](../../../PRD.md#admin-mvp-specification) defines the release boundary. Notifications, analytics, and a role-management UI are deferred.
